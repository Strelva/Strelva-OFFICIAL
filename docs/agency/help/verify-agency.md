# Verify your agency

[Internal 1.0 draft](../README.md). Agency verification and site verification
are separate. **Check my site** proves control of one website; it does not
verify your agency.

## At 1.0

1. Review the status for each effect: publishing to a client domain, Google
   changes, sending email and taking payment. [NOT BUILT — tracked in #254]
2. Provide the evidence requested for the effect you need. The criteria and
   reviewer are still being decided in #233. [NOT BUILT — tracked in #254]
3. Wait for that effect to be verified before using it. Building and previewing
   remain available while you wait. [NOT BUILT — tracked in #255]

A verified agency still needs an active agency relationship and the client's
approval for the action. Payment-provider requirements and account permissions
still apply. Strelva's own agency follows the same verification path.

This revision has no agency-verification screen. Do not substitute an operator
account, a release flag or site-header proof for verification.
