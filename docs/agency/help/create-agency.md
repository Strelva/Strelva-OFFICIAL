# Create your agency workspace

[Internal 1.0 draft](../README.md). An agency workspace keeps your work separate
from each client's business.

## At 1.0

1. Choose **I run an agency** at signup. [NOT BUILT — tracked in #258]
2. Enter your agency name, brand and first client. [NOT BUILT — tracked in #258]
3. Review your verification status for publishing, Google changes, email and
   payments. [NOT BUILT — tracked in #254]

You can build and preview before those effects are verified. Verification does
not replace the client's permission.

## What exists in this revision

A signed-in user with a confirmed email can open **People & access** from a
personal workspace, enter **Agency name**, and select **Create agency workspace**.
That creates the workspace. It does not complete the 1.0 onboarding above.
