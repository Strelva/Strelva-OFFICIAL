# Add a client

[Internal 1.0 draft](../README.md). Have the business name, public website address
and owner's email ready. Confirm that the client wants your agency to do this work.

## At 1.0

1. Add the client from its website URL or a saved prospect. [NOT BUILT — tracked in #259]
2. Check the business details collected from the site and the AI assessment.
   Correct facts with the client before using them. [NOT BUILT — tracked in #259]
3. Choose to connect the existing site or prepare a rebuild. Connecting keeps
   the site's current host. [NOT BUILT — tracked in #259]
4. Give the owner their claim link. Agency-issued owner invitations are a
   separate permission path. [NOT BUILT — tracked in #262]

Owner-invite email also needs agency email verification and release permission.
The silent-rollout email decision remains open in #235; do not promise that an
invite has been sent. A claim link, an assessment handoff and agency access are
different things.

There is no agency URL-add screen in this revision. Do not use `/admin/onboard`
or tenant-conversion scripts as an agency instruction. An accepted assessment
handoff gives the customer a copy; it does not make your agency its provider.
