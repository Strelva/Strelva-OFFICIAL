# Leave or change agency

[Internal 1.0 draft](../README.md). The business keeps control of its data and exit.

<a id="change-provider-at-10"></a>

## Change agency at 1.0

1. As the business owner, choose to replace or end your agency relationship. The outgoing
   agency is told first. [NOT BUILT — tracked in #293]
2. Review the handoff and access changes. An agency change does not transfer
   business ownership. [NOT BUILT — tracked in #293]
3. Request the owner export and agency handoff receipt when needed.
   [NOT BUILT — tracked in #295]

This is different from stopping work in a workspace.

## Current workspace exit

1. As the owner, [export the supported data](./export-data.md) first.
2. On the export page, select **Prepare to leave this workspace**.
3. Choose **Pause new work** or **Cancel new work**.
4. Choose **Keep recorded agency work** or **Revoke future agency work**.
   **Changed by ADR 0013 / decision 7:** these are the target labels; older
   controls may still say "provider work". Engineering must align the copy.
5. Choose **Stop maintained resources** or **Name someone to review them**.
   A named successor must be another verified workspace member. Naming them
   does not grant access or transfer ownership.
6. Read the choices, then select **Save exit choice**. This choice is permanent
   for the workspace.
7. Read the exit results and **Still needs review**, if shown. Use
   **Export retained records** if you need another supported snapshot.

Started, accepted and unresolved results stay available for review. The exit
record does not change billing, prove an outside agency stopped, or switch
agencies. Review subscriptions separately. A connected site stays with its
existing host; that alone does not prove a complete agency handoff.
