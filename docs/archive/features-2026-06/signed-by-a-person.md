# "Signed by a Person" Repositioning

Picked 2026-06-10 (features round, growth side). Not a build — a copy/positioning pass across customer-facing surfaces.

**Type:** human (the AI is the cost structure, not the customer's risk). **Shape:** copy change across marketing site, discovery report, pitch, report footer.

## What it is
Drop "AI-managed" / "AI agent" as customer-facing vocabulary. The customer-facing promise: **run for you, every change approved by you, signed off by a person** (Jacob/Noah by name — "Built in Buffalo" already does this work). AI stays in how it's delivered (chat to make requests is fine — "text us a change" is human language anyway).

## Why
2026-06-10 research: SiteGround force-installed an AI agent on ~1M WordPress sites → 1.1-star revolt; owners actively distrust unattended AI touching their site. Meanwhile "AI-edits-your-site" became platform table stakes (Wix Aria, Airo, B12) — as a label it's now both commoditized AND distrusted. The unclaimed position (Buffalo research, same day): the mechanism — managed updates + weekly proof — not the label.

## Where it touches
- strelva-marketing: home, /features, /trades, /pricing, discovery gate + report copy ("a person reviews every change before it ships").
- Weekly report footer: signed line ("— Jacob, Strelva" style), not "generated by AI".
- Pitch/outbound templates (messaging round 2026-06-10): already mostly compliant; sweep for "AI" as a selling word.
- Design kit voice rules: add the rule so future copy holds the line. Note: design kit principle 1 ("the agent is the product — show its work") still stands for the PRODUCT UI; this repositioning governs SELLING language, not the dashboard's honesty about what the agent did.

## Done = proven
Grep for customer-facing "AI-managed/AI agent" selling copy across marketing surfaces returns only deliberate, mechanism-framed uses; screenshots of changed pages.

## Research basis (dated)
2026-06-10 scouts: SiteGround backlash; chat-edit commoditization (Wix/GoDaddy/B12); Buffalo gaps research (sell mechanism, not label).
