## What you get

1. A custom website, hand-built for your business — not a template you have to learn.
2. Ongoing management: tell the AI what to change (hours, services, photos, posts) and it's handled, with a human reviewing every change before it goes live. One active request at a time.
3. A weekly plain-English report: who found you, what they clicked, what we changed.
4. Monitoring: if your site goes down or something breaks, fixing it is our job.

**Not included (quoted separately when you want them):** redesigns, online stores/checkout, custom features or software, more than one new site section per quarter.

## What you own — always

- **Your domain.** Registered in your name from day one. It is never ours.
- **Your content.** Every word and image. Export it anytime — not just when leaving.
- **On the Managed plan:** after month 12, the complete site — code and files — transfers to you on request, free. Before month 12, you can buy it out anytime: $1,995 minus everything you've paid in subscription so far (never less than $0).

## Leaving

- Cancel with one email. No retention calls, no hoops.
- **Built for you:** the monthly management can end any time after the included 3 months. The site is yours from day one.
- **Managed:** 12-month minimum term, then month-to-month. Leave early via the buyout above. Either way you keep your domain and a full content export.
- We'll send a reminder before any renewal. (Voluntary policy, not a legal recitation.)

## The boring-but-honest parts (lawyer to formalize)

- While subscribed, hosting/deploys run on our infrastructure; on transfer, we hand off per our runbook (repo transfer, content baked in, credentials rotated).
- We never guarantee lead counts, rankings, or AI-recommendation outcomes. We do the work and show you the proof weekly.
- Payment before work starts. Monthly billing via Stripe; 3-day grace on failed payments before access pauses.
- Third-party services connected to your site (booking platforms, etc.) remain governed by their own terms.
