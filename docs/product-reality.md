# Strelva product evidence

## Current decision

Reviewed September 11, 2026. Internal research into horizontal capabilities,
features, and product structure. The [research report](./research/strelva-horizontal-product-research.md)
contains source links, alternatives, constraints, and proposed trials. R&D is
internal. This is a bounded research pass; recurring monitoring is not configured.

Jacob clarified that Strelva is a technology startup with high-growth ambition,
with clients becoming product users under subscription/usage pricing. He rejected
the abstract work-centered and agency-delivery framing as insufficiently exact.
The current exploration keeps four product possibilities open: create working
apps from existing material, run recurring work, share interactive outputs, and
adapt reusable products. Growth through recipients, teams, installations, and
expanded usage is hypothetical. No winner, initial vertical, or managed-service
default was selected. R&D remains internal.

## Evidence corrections

- [Earlier horizontal-versus-vertical research](./strategy/horizontal-vs-vertical-research.md)
  uses verifier agreement as part of confidence. Agreement among agents is not
  independent market evidence. Preserve its historical recommendation as a dated
  argument, not proof against Jacob's later horizontal direction.
- [Earlier IDE memo](./research/ide-for-business-2026-09-11.md) overstates the lack
  of inspection and recovery in alternatives. CL-06 contradicts those categorical
  claims. Owning a runtime can improve control; it is not a universal prerequisite
  for previewing or rehearsing changes to external systems.
- Strelva does not have a validated horizontal completion percentage. The
  [acceptance ledger](./strelvav2-horizontal-acceptance.md) separates local behavior,
  provider evidence, and unimplemented work.
- E-D02 was excluded after verification: the Replit custom-template URL now
  redirects to [Developer Frameworks](https://docs.replit.com/features/project-setup/developer-frameworks),
  whose current text does not establish the investigator's update-propagation
  claim. A Lovable Supabase-specific remix restriction was also removed because
  the current cited page did not establish it. Neither claim supports a product
  decision here.

## What is missing

Current customer interviews, actual product usage paths, paid continuation,
support-inclusive unit economics, representative large-organization constraints,
and practical comparative tests are absent from this pass. Public posts were
purposively selected to expose different workflows, not sampled to estimate
prevalence. No claims about inaccessible private communities are made.

## Revisit triggers

A selected account/product arrangement, a paid trial, a failed workflow, a new
provider capability, or a material change in cost should update affected claims
and alternatives. Keep contradictory evidence linked. Repeat reports of the
same event must not increase independent-user counts. Customer data may enter
internal research only under the applicable access and purpose boundaries.
