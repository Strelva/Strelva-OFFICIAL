Source preparation, October 9. Full rung 5 and ADR 0010/0012; no rate or commercial agreement is selected. The recorded listing remains the accepted, source-qualified listing; maintenance never creates qualification, creator attribution, customer records or access grants.

`/workspace/creator-maintenance?workspaceId=…` is linked from business and agency Billing under the existing `STRELVA_WORKSPACE_RELEASE` and `STRELVA_REVENUE_SPLITS` gates. Current verified creator-workspace owners/admins can read their own listings, immutable maintenance history, and previously recorded creator agreements. The private HTTP command pins the workspace, listing, exact source revision, actual verified session, future effective timestamp and explicit existing agreement/reference. It calls `record_creator_maintenance_from_workspace`, then the unchanged identity-bound `record_creator_royalty_maintenance` command. Identity, listing and current membership lock order is preserved. Takeover uses null agreement/reference; creator/tapered states need their exact existing agreement. Original source, installed Versions, prior invoices, accrued royalty and loss/recovery receipts remain unchanged. The existing natural replay identity is listing plus exact effective time; a conflicting state/agreement for that time is refused. The original writer still requires a future effective time, so a delayed retry after that time must inspect retained history instead of resending.

GET/POST require verified login; POST requires same-origin JSON, an 8000-byte streamed body bound and a per-actor rate limit. Caller actor IDs, monetary rates, unrecognized fields and timezone-free timestamps are refused. Reads and responses remain private/no-store. Command receipts are checked against actor, listing, state, terms and effective instant. A page unmount aborts observation; this does not undo a committed receipt.
