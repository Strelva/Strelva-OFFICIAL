# Strelva visual direction

September 16, 2026. Based on the two boards and critique supplied by Jacob.

This file owns imagery and visual reference interpretation. The
[current direction map](../../DESIGN.md#visual-direction-and-extension-map)
connects it to identity, typography, color, material and motion. September 18
structural review preserves these boards and adds the extension criteria below;
it does not select new art or certify a finished brand system.

Strelva should feel connected to the world people are trying to change: their
businesses, buildings, tools, materials, and work. The imagery gives ordinary
subjects serious attention and makes room for something unfamiliar to happen.

The second board is the main reference. The first remains useful for atmosphere.
Together they establish a direction worth developing. They do not yet establish
a complete or demonstrably distinctive brand system.

## The main reference

![Strelva world board: a bakery, kitchen, workshop, receipt, drawings, hands, material experiments, city views, and Earth. Green objects recur throughout.](./references/strelva-world-2026-09-16.png)

This board brings the idea closer to everyday life. There is food being prepared,
a business lit from inside, a receipt, a workbench, a drawing, and someone making
something. These subjects give the ambition a place to happen.

The glass, stone, paper, steam, and metal matter because they make the images
feel tangible. The shift from a small object to a city or Earth gives the work
a larger horizon. The green object introduces an unexplained possibility.

The board also shows the problem to avoid: that object appears in too many
frames. Repeating it makes the collection easy to connect, but leaves unanswered
whether the images would still feel like Strelva without it.

## The earlier reference

![Strelva atmosphere board: monumental architecture, rough stone, industrial interiors, celestial imagery, landscapes, craft, and shafts of light.](./references/strelva-atmosphere-2026-09-16.png)

The first board establishes darkness, scale, texture, and mystery. Buildings
have weight. Light picks out a surface or an opening. Stone and machinery give
the scenes a physical presence.

It leans more heavily on monumental buildings, cosmic imagery, and spectacle.
Those remain available, but the second board shows how the same atmosphere can
support a kitchen, receipt, or workshop. Future work needs that broader range.

Both images are preserved here as unchanged copies of Jacob's attachments.
Their lettering and depicted businesses are reference material, not approved
brand copy or evidence of actual customers.

## What we are trying to express

The critique Jacob supplied describes the intent directly:

> Human ambition is photographed with gravity.
> Useful work is physical.
> Experimentation leaves evidence.
> Something new occasionally enters the world.
> We don't fully explain it.

The emotional aim is that ordinary people deserve extraordinary capability.
That should be visible in the attention given to their work. A restaurant
opening deserves care in its depiction. So does a difficult legal process, a
machine under development, or a small team attempting something large.

“Useful work is physical” is an image-making premise here. Software and
intellectual work also belong in Strelva. Their people, settings, documents,
experiments, and consequences offer ways to show them without relying on
abstract technology imagery.

## What connects the images

The boards share a subdued palette: charcoal, dark brown, grey stone, olive,
sage, and warm cream. Amber light brings warmth to the businesses and interiors.
Green is muted enough to sit among those materials, even when it becomes strange.
These are observed color relationships, not final color values.

Lighting does much of the work. Window light, task lamps, lit storefronts, and
narrow beams give the viewer something specific to look at. Deep shadow creates
quiet around it. Haze and grain contribute atmosphere, but neither needs to be
applied to every image.

Surfaces retain detail: creased paper, rough stone, worked metal, glass, food,
and dust. People appear through hands and concentrated activity. Drawings,
documents, and experiments can sit beside photographic scenes because they
show another part of the same effort.

The framing moves between close observation and distance. A hand holding an
object and a city seen across water need not have the same composition to
belong together. Sparse typography leaves the images room. The tiny captions
in the boards do not establish a usable type system.

## Use the symbols sparingly

Jacob's supplied critique suggests explicit green objects in roughly 10–20% of
imagery. Use that as an initial guide across a collection, not a quota for each
page. Sometimes only a consequence is visible. Sometimes nothing strange happens.

Literal stacked stones should appear even less often. The logo already carries
them. Repeating the logo's subject throughout the photography risks making each
scene feel arranged to explain the brand.

When a phenomenon does appear, it can remain unexplained. It does not need a
story about what the sphere represents or how it powers Strelva. Its scarcity
should make the appearance matter.

## The range still needs to be proven

The supplied critique names five stories this direction needs to accommodate:

- A local restaurant opening.
- A manufacturer building a new machine.
- A law firm improving a painful process.
- A three-person startup attempting something enormous.
- A future company building infrastructure.

These are subjects to explore, not campaigns already designed. Each needs its
own people, objects, activity, and setting. They should not all acquire the same
green moon, old factory, or dramatic skyline.

A useful additional test would be a bright contemporary office or clean
manufacturing floor. The boards strongly favor dark, weathered environments.
We have not yet shown how far their visual character can travel beyond those
settings. Artificially aging every business would narrow the world unnecessarily.

The direction has coherence. Its distinctiveness is still an ambition. A
collection with fewer symbols and more varied subjects will give us a better
basis for judging whether it is becoming recognizably Strelva.

## What this means for the interface

The boards guide art direction. They do not prescribe a screen layout, navigation,
component shape, animation, or final font. Those decisions still need to be made
against real content and tasks under the [product design contract](../../DESIGN.md).

The current Home direction uses a dark palette and an illustrated business.
This imagery can inform that business's presence and its surroundings. Text,
controls, records, and states still need clear contrast and readable detail.
Photographic darkness, grain, haze, and tiny lettering should not obscure work.
The earlier September 9 ivory Home palette remains a historical direction.

Public marketing implementation belongs in the sibling marketing repository.
Client sites keep their own identities. These boards have not been implemented
as a complete product treatment, and the documents do not claim otherwise.

## How to judge the next work

### Landing imagery placement: September 16

Jacob rejected using imagery as the landing-page background. Photographs and
illustrations belong in separate content regions with visible boundaries and
space around them. Keep headings, captions, fields, and actions outside those
regions on solid surfaces. Do not fade imagery into the page or place interface
controls over it. This supersedes the earlier continuous Buffalo background for
the next landing design, without claiming a change to the implemented site.

The two references supplied with this correction are preserved unchanged:

- [Material-world board](./references/strelva-material-world-2026-09-16.png):
  people at work, paper, stone, tools, buildings, landscapes, and larger scales.
- [Architectural illustration](./references/strelva-watercolor-architecture-2026-09-16.png):
  fine ink linework, watercolor texture, navy and sage washes, and warm masonry.

Use both references. The architectural drawing expands the available media;
it does not require every subject to become a watercolor or a Buffalo landmark.
Exact compositions, copy, and type choices in generated mockups remain proposals.

### Judging the collection

Look at the images together, including the ordinary ones. Ask whether the
subject is specific, whether the materials and activity are believable, and
whether the image holds attention without a recurring symbol. Then ask whether
the collection can move between different businesses and scales while retaining
its character.

The later foundation decisions select Geist, custom logo lettering and the
component color/motion contracts linked from the current direction map. Those
are not open font choices in this imagery study. Image grading, the balance of
dark and light settings, and exact visual compositions remain reviewable. The
immediate creative challenge is to establish that range while making the green
object and stones less necessary for recognition.

## Visual review and extension

Use the same meaningful content to compare an existing treatment with a proposed
enhancement. Show the actual intended size and setting, not only a large isolated
crop. The criteria below connect art direction to usable product surfaces;
they do not select a new page layout.

| Review area | What the comparison must make judgeable |
| --- | --- |
| Identity | Original cairn and approved lettering remain recognizable at small and large sizes. Optional motion has a complete static result. |
| Typography | Selected Geist roles, readable hierarchy and realistic long text. Logo artwork stays distinct from arbitrary text. Weight choices are shown on the actual intended material. |
| Color and material | Both independently tuned themes, clear semantic states, legible content at rest and during motion, and an opaque fallback. A comfortable light card cannot be inferred from a dark specimen. |
| Composition | The main work is easy to locate; spacing and grouping express relationships. Test sparse and dense content without making every region an identical card. |
| Imagery | Specific people, activity and materials; range across the supplied business examples; coherence without needing a recurring green object or stone motif. Reference scenes never become customer proof. |
| Motion | The transition explains a state change or a deliberately selected identity moment. Interrupted and reduced-motion states retain clear content and available controls. |
| Use | Desktop and narrow layouts, long labels, enlarged text and relevant error/loading/empty states. Visual polish must survive actual work, not only a clean presentation frame. |

Keep original references unchanged under `references/`. Put exploratory derivatives
in the existing relevant prototype directory. In that study's existing record,
identify the source, creator or generation method, date, intended use, rights or
permission status where applicable, and whether the result is a reference,
proposal or selected asset. A reference's depicted logo or business is not an
asset license or a relationship claim.

For each comparison, state which property is being explored and which direction
is held constant. Preserve materially different options when taste is unresolved.
Record Jacob's selection with its scope in the owning contract; keep rejected
or superseded studies clearly labeled so they cannot return as defaults.
Promotion into code follows the [foundation extension contract](../component-system.md#extending-the-foundation),
with actual consumer adoption and dated evidence recorded separately.
