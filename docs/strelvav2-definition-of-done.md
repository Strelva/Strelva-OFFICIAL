# What finished means for strelvav2

Status: proposed acceptance contract, prepared from Jacob's horizontal direction
and ten-part product learning loop. This defines work to accept; it does not
certify implementation or authorize production. It supersedes screen-count
percentages and the unsupported 55% horizontal estimate.

## The promise

Strelva helps a person or organization discover useful work, create what is
needed, and delegate its operation. The person can do it themselves, ask Strelva
for help, or involve an agency without moving the work or losing its history.
Internal R&D discovers which offerings deserve to exist, tests them, and learns
whether they create customer value. Both use the same work, permissions,
responsibilities, receipts, and costs.

Finished means these loops work across a defined set of different jobs. It does
not mean every possible product, industry, integration, or future model exists.
New discoveries enter a subsequent release or an explicit scope revision. They
cannot silently change the denominator or grant authority to build and publish.

## Three distinct finish lines

The September 14 [architecture clarification](./horizontal-product-brief-2026-09-11.md#september-14-architecture-clarification)
and [source audit](./horizontal-audit-and-plan-2026-09-11.md) add connected
acceptance for explicit business ownership, focused recipient use, uninterrupted
application releases, custom-code delivery and versioned capability expansion.
These are mandatory target behaviors, not polish left after screen completion.
Earlier local evidence remains valid only for the narrower behavior it tested.

| Finish line | Acceptance | What cannot count as proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Horizontal interface | The behavioral surfaces below compose across the workspace, focused finished experiences and internal console, with clear states for future work | Screen counts, nine independently built interface families, duplicate inquiry screens, or links to empty fixtures |
| Local product and learning system | All 20 customer-product requirements in the existing ledger and all ten learning requirements below pass their agreed journeys using durable authenticated local state | Mock adapters presented as integrations, model prose presented as execution, or Coming soon on a required behavior |
| Operated and qualified offering | Separately authorized provider operation and pilots establish reliability, support-inclusive economics, repeat use, and recovery for that offering | Deployment, synthetic users, test counts, or estimated savings |

The current production hold permits work toward the first two. Provider-specific
proof and customer outcomes stay visibly pending. Do not describe a locally
complete system as a proven commercial product. A working learning system can
correctly conclude that an offering should be stopped.

## What owners, staff and Strelva each use

Staff and recipients enter only the application, document, form or decision they
are authorized to use. They do not inherit the creator's workspace or its design
controls. Creation, ordinary use and ongoing operation share resource identity
and evidence while enforcing their different permissions. Internal R&D remains
inside the separate internal environment. The nine interface families are views
of these shared records and operations, not nine permanent sidebar destinations.

The workspace shell uses a context switcher, Home, search, New and pinned working
destinations named for the actual resource, such as Website or Staff availability.
Browse holds the full inventory; account and administration remain accessible.
R&D is an internal workspace with access controls. Findings appear as useful
suggestions or decisions beside the affected work.
Evidence, alternatives, costs, and research methods are available when inspected.
Research on Strelva itself must never combine private customer evidence without
its permitted purpose and scope. Agency methods stay private by default.

R&D is internal, as Jacob explicitly clarified. It can reuse shared work and
governance mechanisms while retaining separate access, evidence, and acceptance
records. It is not a customer offering. Customer readiness and internal research
maturity must remain separately inspectable even when this release includes both.
The [product research](./research/strelva-horizontal-product-research.md) explores
capabilities and product structure; its options do not become release requirements
until selected and added to the acceptance contract.

## Horizontal screen acceptance

These are behavioral gates, not separate products. Shared presentation does not
require storing every domain in one generic table or replacing native policies.
The following historical screen names are a coverage checklist, not independent
engineering assignments or a required navigation layout. Accept the connected
journeys in the architecture audit before declaring the shared product complete.
Unsupported future offerings may have useful, clearly labeled Coming soon
views; they are not installed capabilities and cannot appear in What's live.

| Screen | What must be true before it passes |
| --- | --- |
| Sidebar | Same compact navigation for every work type; switching businesses retains correct access and clears stale selection; agencies see their permitted clients |
| Business home | Needs you, Strelva is handling, What changed, What's live aggregate supported work in that order; each row opens the exact item; pause and Undo use its actual authority; no unsolicited charts |
| New | Useful context and examples mean users need not know feature names; an editable proposal distinguishes supported work, missing inputs, and Coming soon before execution |
| Work | Request, shape, plan, editable result, receipt, and release action stay together; direct edits and language requests share change history; private work uses Save or Apply when publishing is irrelevant |
| Capability | Records, details, and editable plain-language rules use a common structure across work types; native schemas and validation remain authoritative |
| Record | The selected object, its links, timeline, and actor history survive reload, new activity, and authorized handoff |
| Inspector | Selection-scoped details, history, permitted actions, and an 'on this' request work across supported objects; bulk changes yield one receipt with conflict-aware Undo; mobile opens an accessible panel |
| Rehearsal | Synthetic activity is unmistakable and isolated; saved checks bind to exact versions and rerun after relevant changes; unsupported provider checks cannot silently pass |
| Receipt | Actual before/after presentation fits the object; versions, actor, reason, sources, result, and recovery are retained; new records survive rollback |
| Why | Answers trace recorded events across supported jobs; missing evidence stays missing; a fix is offered as a proposal; correlation is not represented as causation |
| Responsibility | A readable document governs scope, allowed/forbidden actions, approvals, hours, budget, escalation, trust, pause, and cancellation across supported job types |
| Connections | Read/write scope, consent, freshness, errors, task access, and disconnect reflect actual grants; revocation is checked before the next action |
| Onboarding | Supplied website, documents, or existing workspace context yield sourced statements that can be corrected; unavailable sources are visible; users can begin without a website |
| Agency | Scoped client access, cross-client Attention, contributions, review, and reusable Patterns share the customer experience; installation never copies records, secrets, or grants |

An action already executed in the outside world may be irreversible. Receipts
must distinguish reversing a configuration, compensating a transaction, and an
action with no inverse. Sending an email cannot acquire an Undo promise merely
because the surrounding workflow has one.

## Minimum breadth that proves the horizontal promise

The release needs the following concrete journeys. Different names on the same
inquiry form do not establish breadth. All must reopen in the common workspace.

| Job family | Acceptance example |
| --- | --- |
| Customer requests | A service business receives a quote request, creates the record, routes it, and follows up within its approved rules |
| Records and coordination | A project tracker accepts new records, links related work, assigns responsibility, supports grouped edits, and protects later changes during Undo |
| Knowledge and documents | Selected sources produce a reviewable document; corrections, source revisions, and private sharing survive reload |
| Scheduling | A booking finds permitted availability, handles a conflict, requests approval where needed, creates the reservation, and recovers safely from a provider interruption |
| Ongoing investigation | A recurring job compares records from two authorized systems, investigates a discrepancy, and asks only about the unresolved part; no change also produces an honest result |
| Interfaces and applications | A bounded portal or application composes approved parts, applies domain permissions, runs in isolation, shows evidence, and has a named maintenance and deployment owner |
| People and outside agents | A customer hands bounded work to an agency or sponsored agent; the result is reviewed, costs are attributed, and revocation stops new access and actions |

Exercise these across independent businesses and an organization with restricted
members. Use creation, ongoing operation, and recovery examples, not only happy
paths. An enterprise label additionally requires a selected identity lifecycle,
policy, access-review, audit/export, retention, and operating contract. Exact
enterprise commitments remain open; the broad enterprise gate cannot pass until
they are specified and demonstrated.

## The ten-part learning loop

These ten requirements extend the existing R&D comparison work. They are not
claims that ten autonomous services already run. One internal project must be
traceable through the entire loop, including a rejected idea and a changed mind.

| ID and part | Required output | Acceptance example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R1 Reality | A recurring, budgeted research responsibility produces source-linked claims with capture time, event date, excerpt or permitted reference, segment, freshness, and contrary evidence | The next scheduled run processes new material, detects a changed or withdrawn source, updates affected claims, records cost, and exposes source outages; duplicate posts do not become independent observations |
| R2 Problem discovery | An opportunity traces evidence to repeated behavior, constraint, unresolved job, and possible value, retaining OBSERVED, INFERRED, and UNKNOWN | A reviewer can follow every asserted behavior to its source and contest the inference; unsupported and contradictory findings remain visible |
| R3 User understanding | Interviews, support records, and permitted behavioral data support quotes, segments, journeys, contradictions, and unanswered questions | A claim about a segment lists its actual sample and limits; simulated objections remain separate and cannot increase demand confidence or participant counts |
| R4 Frontier capability | A dated assessment explains what changed, which workflow may now be feasible, previous limits, remaining constraints, and likely economics | A vendor release prompts a bounded trial on a held-out workflow; measured completion, correction, cost, and latency stay distinct from vendor claims and public benchmarks |
| R5 Possibilities | Several materially different responses attach to evidence, capability, advantage, and required behavior change | Compare an integration into existing work, a workflow removal/redesign, and a new interface or service, plus doing nothing; cosmetic variants do not count as divergent options |
| R6 Strategy | Each serious proposal addresses value, behavior, alternatives, generic-model substitution, distribution, activation, retention, compounding, defensibility, payer, market effects, future AI, and kill criteria | The recommendation names its weakest assumption and a falsifying test; an idea can be parked or rejected without generating a build task |
| R7 Coherence | A versioned product brief defines audience, promise, domain objects, boundaries, and accepted tradeoffs | Review can reject a feature, put it inside an existing object, remove a weak feature, or merge duplicate behavior; any new object or navigation destination needs a concrete reason |
| R8 Experience | Goal-based browser/computer sessions retain objective, role, conditions, actions, dead ends, completion, and observed friction | An unfamiliar-user objective and restricted-member recovery objective run without button scripts; desktop/mobile/keyboard and empty/large/error states are covered as relevant; agent hesitation is not human usability evidence |
| R9 Build and verify | Intent, behavior contract, implementation, focused verification, independent review, and release decision remain linked | A defect found after implementation changes the result or blocks acceptance; tests written by the builder alone cannot establish completion; migrations, access, recovery, performance, and observability receive proportionate checks |
| R10 Reality after launch | Expected versus observed outcomes, cohort definitions, source events, exposure windows, uncertainty, costs, and a next test feed back into claims and priorities | A changed outcome revises or retires an opportunity and triggers a governed experiment proposal; absent telemetry says unknown; simulated events exercise the mechanism but never prove retention or value |

### Research coverage and limits

Each project selects relevant competitors, adjacent products, startups, user
segments, workflows, and technologies. Its source registry can cover App Store
and Chrome reviews, Reddit, Hacker News, X, forums and permitted Discord/community
material, GitHub issues, support forums, YouTube, customer reviews, industry
reports, job postings, procurement documents, pricing, API/model/platform
releases, papers, MCPs/connectors, regulation, distribution, and behavior shifts.

Each enabled source has an access method, permitted purpose, refresh cadence,
freshness limit, spend limit, and failure policy. Unsupported sources are labeled
Coming soon; inaccessible sources are unavailable. Neither is silently counted
as covered. Do not bypass access restrictions or start paid subscriptions.
Continuous means durable scheduled and change-triggered work with pause and
recovery, not reading every source on every turn. Deduplicate public research
where permitted; never pool private customer or agency context by default.

The capability radar covers reasoning, computer/browser use, vision/video/audio,
speech/realtime, tool use, context/memory, agent coordination, coding, local
models, inference price and latency, structured output, APIs, connectors, open
source libraries, and robotics where relevant. A newly available tool is an
input to a task-specific feasibility test, not a permission grant or reliable
offering. A model, dependency, or connector update may invalidate prior proof.

### Evidence rules

Source counts, distinct authors, distinct organizations, and independent events
are separate quantities. If independence cannot be established, say so. Twenty-nine
mentions across four channels do not prove twenty-nine independent users, a
representative market, or a motive. The supplied Sheets example is illustrative
until its actual sources are collected.

Keep a source assertion separate from directly observed behavior. A review is
evidence that its author reported a workflow; it is not an observed session.
Confidence describes a specific claim and cites relevance, directness,
independence, consistency, recency, and sampling limits. A model's self-rating
or repeated agreement is insufficient. Counterexamples and source retractions
must propagate to dependent opportunities and decisions without erasing history.

Synthetic users generate objections and test hypotheses. They do not establish
customer demand, willingness to pay, retention, or market prevalence. External
content is untrusted data, including any embedded instructions. Research cannot
silently publish, email, change grants, or spend beyond the approved scope.

Retention differences between cohorts are associations until a credible design
supports causation. Experiment records name assignment, exposure, outcome,
measurement window, exclusions, sample limits, and guardrails before examining
results. An agent may propose a randomized rollout; authority to observe does
not authorize changing a customer's experience.

## Acceptance evidence and progress reporting

Track three separate sets of requirements: the horizontal behavior checklist, the
[20 customer-product requirements](./strelvav2-horizontal-acceptance.md), and R1–R10.
Do not average them into a persuasive but meaningless product percentage.
The September 14 architecture cases extend the relevant product requirements;
they must be explicitly accepted and cannot inherit a prior bounded-app pass.

Each requirement has stable acceptance cases, owner, implementation link,
evidence link, tested version/environment, remaining gap, and reviewer decision.
Its state is: not audited, missing, shell only, implemented/unverified, or
accepted. A percentage is accepted cases divided by the predeclared cases within
that named gate. Missing and unverified cases earn no acceptance credit, but
their implementation progress is still reported. Publish the count and scope
revision alongside the percentage. Never change weights or split completed
tasks to inflate it. A scope addition shows both the old and revised baseline.

100% of local v2 requires all mandatory cases in the three local checklists
(screens, customer product, and learning), no unresolved access, data-loss,
duplicate-action, or cost-limit defects, and a reviewed local
release record. 95% can leave bounded polish only; it cannot leave a whole engine,
provider class, or permission model missing. Unknown acceptance parameters remain
open cases. Coming soon can satisfy an honest future-offering display case, but
cannot satisfy that offering's working behavior.

Jacob owns human end-to-end acceptance. Focused command tests and selected
goal-based agent sessions reduce his review burden; they do not substitute for
his decision or real user research. Avoid exhaustive browser permutations and
repeated broad suites. Prioritize dangerous failures and jobs that challenge a
shared abstraction. Feature-complete, locally accepted, provider-verified, and
commercially proven remain different statuses.

Pilot success criteria are agreed before collecting outcome evidence: accepted
work quality, completion and recovery rates, time to first value, human minutes
including setup/review/correction/support/maintenance, total tool cost, usage and
repeat value, plus cancellation reasons. Labor capacity and actual cash savings
are different; completed tasks do not establish revenue attribution. Numeric
thresholds, prices, source budgets, pilot sample/windows, and enterprise service
terms are still decisions, not facts selected by this document.

## Sequence and current implementation limits

The [September 14 build order](./horizontal-audit-and-plan-2026-09-11.md#build-order-and-acceptance-cases)
owns engineering sequence. Jacob's latest gate is one complete application:
request, creation, owner use, sharing, employee use, accumulated records,
proposed change, exact review, publication, recovery and continued use. The live
application remains the object throughout. Complete and review that experience
before extending the interaction pattern to documents, websites or other work.
The broader delivery, operation, reuse and capability requirements remain open
until separately demonstrated. Finish existing correctness fixes and keep
internal failure visibility alongside the real runs.

The internal learning acceptance artifact remains a versioned trace from source evidence
through a rejected alternative, selected experiment, reviewed result, and changed
decision after new evidence. Persistent access, owned state, historical context,
cross-system coordination, and an ongoing assignment are possible advantages
over a generic model. Each proposal must establish which advantage it actually
needs; where a normal model conversation finishes the job equally well, recommend
that simpler route rather than inventing a product around it.

The following requirements remain part of the program; their order does not
override the architecture build order above.

5. Exercise all seven job families and all ten learning stages, including

The September 12 implementation now includes bounded cross-capability execution,
native private app output, local scheduling/investigation, scoped contributions,

[acceptance ledger](./strelvav2-horizontal-acceptance.md) records tested behavior
and remaining gaps. Local state transitions and API fixtures do not establish a
live autonomous research service, actual postlaunch outcomes, provider-wide
reliability or an accepted enterprise offering. Jacob's review and exact provider
activation remain separate. This contract selects no new database or workflow
vendor and does not relocate client-specific apps into this repository.

## Research checked for this definition

The cited CHI study reports stronger agreement with population response
distributions than with specific individuals in its interview-grounded concept
testing setting. This supports treating simulation as exploratory screening,
not replacing individual customer evidence. It does not prove all synthetic
research useless. [Wang and Siu, CHI 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29890).

The longitudinal coding-agent study reports persistent increases in static
warnings and cognitive complexity in its open-source sample, with heterogeneous
throughput effects. That motivates independent verification; it does not prove
that agents inevitably degrade every repository or that a particular architecture
prevents it. [Agarwal, He, and Vasilescu, MSR 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13597).
