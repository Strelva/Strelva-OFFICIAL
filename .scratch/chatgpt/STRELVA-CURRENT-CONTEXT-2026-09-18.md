# Strelva current context

Prepared September 18, 2026 for Jacob's Strelva ChatGPT project.

Use this as the current reference for product, interface and image exploration. It replaces `STRELVA-UI-UX-CONTEXT-2026-09-15.md`. Later explicit direction from Jacob takes precedence. Older conversations and generated concepts are historical evidence, not accepted decisions.

## What Strelva is becoming

Strelva is moving from being hired for a website or defined project toward a product businesses use to improve and operate their work. Its aim is to make ambition affordable: help people accomplish work that was previously too expensive, slow or difficult while preserving their judgment, authority and relationships.

Someone brings Strelva a business need. The result may be software, a document, analysis, a change in an existing system or ongoing help. Improving work that already exists and enabling previously unaffordable work are equally part of the direction. Replacement of an existing tool or provider depends on the offering; it is not the default.

The ambition is a broadly adopted technology product for solo owners through large organizations. Managed Websites is the established commercial and operating base, not the product's ceiling. Agencies can use Strelva, operate offerings for clients and help develop new ones. They are one audience within the broader product.

Self-service, Strelva-managed help and outside-agency collaboration can change around the same work. A person can ask for advice, request review, delegate a task or arrange ongoing responsibility while keeping the work's context and history.

The business is the enduring home. An offering combines usable software, completed work or ongoing help into a specific promise. An installation configures an offering for one business with its version, connected resources and accepted responsibility. An assignment gives a person or agent permission to operate specified work within agreed limits. Documents and one-off work can remain independently useful. These distinctions should guide behavior without becoming unnecessary interface jargon.

## People and authority

Owners open their business and use its applications, documents and websites. Changes, help, review and history belong beside the thing being used.

Employees should open the permitted application directly. Customers should receive the form, document, website or decision they need. Neither should have to enter the creator's workspace to use the result.

Agencies need a portfolio of explicitly shared client work and a clear transition into each independent business. Strelva delivery staff need their accepted assignments. Platform operators and internal R&D use separate internal surfaces.

One person can be owner, payer, creator, approver or operator, but these roles do not automatically grant one another's permissions. Client businesses keep their records when an agency relationship ends. Reusable methods must not carry customer data into another business.

Spending, publishing, contacting people, sharing private information and destructive actions require explicit authority. A connection is not permission for every action. Requesting a provider does not mean the provider accepted responsibility. Show partial results, failed verification and recoverable failure honestly.

## Experience direction

Everyone uses one persistent Strelva environment: free and paid users, agencies, managed clients and enterprise members. The shared business navigation is Home, Work, Ongoing, People & access, and Settings. New, Search, Explore offerings and Help are utilities. Personal Account is separate from business Settings. These labels are the selected baseline, not a requirement that every concept reproduce the same layout.

The ChatGPT comparison means approachable entry, familiar persistent navigation and easy return to work. It does not require chat to be the main object. Conversation is useful when it helps someone express or investigate a need. A form, document, record collection, calendar or comparison should use the working surface its task needs.

People should receive value without knowing the perfect prompt or completing organization setup first. Begin from a recognizable need, useful example, file, link or permitted business context. Introduce setup and connections when the work requires them.

The core change journey is business -> work -> live thing -> proposed change -> review -> live thing. Keep the current result usable while an improvement is prepared. Publishing and recovery must preserve later customer records. Customers should not have to operate deployments, execution jobs, agent graphs or database structures to complete an ordinary change.

Five actions recur across offerings: use business information, make something usable, put it to work, change how it works, and work with people or external AI.

## Home and public entry

Home places an illustrated business at the center with actual work arranged around it. The building, dark palette and restrained motion remain selected. The illustration represents the selected business context; it must not pretend to be a photograph or accurate model of a customer's property. On mobile, work cards become a usable list below the illustration.

A public visitor can bring a business name, website, example, file or connected source and discover a concrete opportunity. A preview may show found public information and a few specific improvements before signup, but public identification does not establish ownership or access to private work. Preserve the chosen business and result through signup, refresh and return.

## Current visual direction

The original three-stone cairn and custom Strelva lettering form the identity. The lettering is seven-letter vector artwork, not a typeface for other words. Use Geist Sans for interface and display text. Inter and Fraunces describe legacy source and historical comparisons, not the forward typography direction.

The visual world combines people, businesses, materials and evidence of work. Deep ink and blue-green atmosphere, irregular moss light, fine grain and warm ivory establish character. Unusual phenomena should be selective. Literal stacked stones should be rarer still. Green objects can appear in roughly 10-20% of an image collection as an initial editorial guide, not a quota.

Use semantic color roles and finish light and dark treatments independently. The existing dark family includes deep graphite and forest-tinted surfaces, warm ivory text, muted sage accents, quiet rules and soft depth. Do not treat extracted values as a second design specification or force the product palette onto client websites.

Prominent gloss and selective atmospheric material are part of the foundation. Atmospheric cards use a continuous frosted surface over bounded material, with sharp text and controls. Do not paste opaque inner cards onto a decorative background unless the content requires containment. Substantial cards use 24px outer padding and 24px corners; controls use 12px corners. Exact pigment, grain, reflections, light-card treatment and Geist weights remain reviewable.

Imagery belongs in separate content regions, never behind landing-page text or controls. Product composition should follow the task. Atmosphere, containment and expressive type do not prescribe one page template.

Motion should explain relationships, preserve orientation or acknowledge an action. It must be interruptible, respect reduced motion and never simulate business activity. Ordinary navigation should keep the identity static unless a deliberate interaction calls for the existing logo morph.

## Working from the component foundation

Start with semantic roles and owned atoms, then compose the surface. Reuse the existing Button, IconButton, Toggle, Card, field family and Tabs implementations. Extend an owning primitive when its contract is missing; do not rebuild its appearance, focus, sizing or motion in page CSS.

The component system is only partly adopted. A working gallery, source implementation or passing test does not prove that every consumer migrated, that Jacob accepted the aesthetic or that the work shipped. Keep recorded direction, implementation, rendered proof, human acceptance and deployment separate.

Marketing owns its components and public composition. Client repositories own their brand-led presentation. Similar colors, radii or blur do not create a shared component package.

## Image and concept guidance

Show a person, business context, immediate task and state. Give the actual result enough room. Use plausible, readable content and make the next consequential action visible.

When useful, explore a short sequence: use an application, inspect a record, propose a change, review the consequence and return to the live result. Include mobile, empty, loading, disconnected, permission-limited or recoverable-error states when they reveal the design.

Create materially different alternatives when asked. A color swap is not a new direction. Explain what changes for the user and why it may be better. A generated concept remains a proposal until Jacob selects it.

Treat fictional business names, numbers and activity as concept data. Do not imply real customers, revenue, integrations or successful actions. An attractive image does not prove behavior, accessibility or readiness.

Native components, custom applications and work inside existing systems are all available product material. Generated or temporary interfaces are useful when they make the task easier, provided stable identity, ownership, data, controls and recovery remain clear.

## Commercial and evidence boundaries

The selected pricing direction is generous subscriptions with useful included work, understandable usage and customer spending limits. Higher subscriptions may include bounded human help. Exact prices, allowances and metering remain open. Do not invent commercial promises or imply unlimited delivery.

Local `strelvav2` work includes the shared frame, business Home, applications, documents, trackers, ongoing work, offerings, access, budgets and internal learning. Implementation and verification vary by path. The component foundation is also incomplete. There is no audited whole-product completion claim.

Local tests, browser fixtures and previews do not prove production operation, adoption, customer value or unrestricted autonomous delivery. The horizontal interface is not established as a production release. Managed Websites remains the existing commercial base under its own agreements.

REB owns the Managed Websites control plane and current common customer interface. `strelva-marketing` owns public presentation. Client websites have separate repositories and brand-led presentation. This repository does not settle a public Custom Software offering.
