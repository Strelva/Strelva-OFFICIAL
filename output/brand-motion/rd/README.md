# Strelva R&D mark

Jacob selected the three-ribbon spiral around the sage dot for internal Strelva team R&D use on September 9, 2026.

`strelva-rd.svg` is the standalone vector mark, extracted from the settled final geometry of the 20-second reveal in `../v3/strelva-field-20s.mp4`. It omits the film's particles and registration lines. Its three ribbons retain their separate identities from the original three pills. The mark is transparent; ribbon color uses `currentColor` with an ink default, and the dot uses sage.

This is an internal R&D identity. It does not replace the main three-stone Strelva logo or establish a public product, division, or service offering.
